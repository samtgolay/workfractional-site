About

I'm Sam, nice to meet you.

My squiggly-line-generalist career, curiosity, and passion for working with business owners brought me here. I spent over a decade in corporate: B2B sales at Nestlé, executive recruiting in an agency and in-house for Netflix and Google, then leading operations and expansion inside venture-backed startups. I went fractional because, frankly, I wanted the autonomy to work across industries and do great work without the bureaucracy.

Along the way I met founders and CEOs who wanted senior-level results but weren't ready for a full-time exec. WorkFractional is how I scale that. I lead the relationship and the GTM work, backed by a vetted bench of ops, recruiting, and GTM specialists. You hire one operator, you get the team behind her.

We help founders build it with strong operational foundations, scale it with sustainable processes, and exit it cleanly with the right people and systems in place. The goal is always the same: leave your team stronger than we found it.
