About

I'm Sam, founder of WorkFractional.

I spent the first chapter of my career in corporate B2B sales, then executive recruiting, then leading operations inside venture-backed companies. I've sat in every seat of the founder's problem: the pipeline that isn't compounding, the first sales hire that didn't land, the ops stack held together with goodwill.

I went fractional because the founders I most wanted to work with weren't ready for a full-time exec. They needed senior thinking now, embedded in their team, with a clear end date.

WorkFractional is how I scale that. I lead the relationship and the GTM work. I'm backed by a vetted bench of senior operators in recruiting and operations who plug in when scope calls for it. You hire one operator, you get a team behind her.

We help founders build it, scale it, exit it. That means a real system on the way in, a real handoff on the way out, and your team stronger than we found it.
