Set Expectations

Full transparency.

90-DAY BLOCKS

Engagements are 90-day blocks, renewable. This gives both sides a real review point. We rescope or move on at the end of each block.

ALONGSIDE, NOT ABOVE

I work alongside your team, not above it. I plug into your tools and your standups. I don't replace your existing leaders, I make them more effective.

THE BENCH

Bench operators come in when scope calls for it. Every engagement starts with me. When the work expands beyond my lane, I bring in the right specialist from the bench, vetted and accountable to the same outcome.

FAQ

Questions founders ask.

How quickly can we start?

Typically within two weeks of our Discovery Call, depending on scope.

What if I'm not sure which tier I need?

Start with the Operations Diagnostic. The roadmap tells us where to begin.

No. That’s what the Diagnostic is for. Most founders come in with a symptom, slow sales, a bad hire, chaos in delivery, and we find the system underneath it.

Can I switch tiers between blocks?

Yes. We rescope at the end of each 90-day block.

Do you work with my existing team?

Yes. I embed in your tools, attend your standups, and partner with your leaders. I never go around them.

Is there a contract minimum?

Each retainer block is a 90-day commitment. After that, renewable in 90-day blocks.

Do you sign NDAs?

Always.
